Admission Criteria

(Nursery - IX, XI)

Age Criteria: Nursery, Prep I, Prep II & Grade 1

The age of the applicant as on 31st July 2027, as recorded in the municipal birth certificate, will determine the eligibility for admission to a particular class.

Recommended Grades for children born between the following dates:

Grade Age (In Years) Born Between
Grade Nursery Three years + 01/08/2023 – 31/07/2024
Grade Preparatory I Four years + 01/08/2022 – 31/07/2023
Grade Preparatory II Five years + 01/08/2021 – 31/07/2022
Grade 1 Six years + 01/08/2020 – 31/07/2021

For grade II onwards — Refer below given table. The enquiry and registration process are only through online mode, there is no requirement of the applicant or parent to be physically present at the City Office or School campus for the same. However, parents are welcome to visit office or school for their understanding or need.

Steps for Enquiry, Registration & Admission for Nursery onwards
Activity Mode Fees Requirement
Enquiry Online Free No Documents Required
Registration Online Rs. 9,900/-(Non Refundable) Payment online only Candidate's Documents : Passport size color picture (JPEG/JPG) and Report card of last qualified class(from grade VI onwards).
Parents' Documents :Passport size color picture (JPEG/JPG).
*(All documents size shall be less than 1 MB in PDF/JPEG/JPG formats.)
Admission to Nursery, Prep I & Prep II Candidate to meet required Age Criteria
Admission for Grade I - III Interaction with the child Informal Interaction with the child
Grade IV – IX and XI Offline Written test and Interaction. SYLLABUS
Admission Results Online/SMS
Completion of Admission formalities Offline Online payment of prescribed fee & e-NACH Registration • Original School leaving certificate & Report card of last qualified class – (Mandatory from Grade II onwards. Students coming from outside Agra needs the TC to be countersigned by respective Education officer/authority)
• Self-attested colored photocopy of Birth Certificate by the Municipal Corporation for Nur, Prep I, Prep II & Grade 1.
• Health card(Provided by the School) to be duly signed by a registered Medical Practitioner.
• Recent individual passport size color photographs of the candidate and the parent.
Note:
1. Admission will be cancelled if any variance or misrepresentation is found between the particulars filled in the Registration Form and the supporting documents at any stage.
2. The school reserves its exclusive right to select/reject an application. It is expected from the parent that all the details should be as per the documents applicable for the respective class.
3. For Nursery, Prep I, and Prep II admissions, as the school does not conduct prior interaction or assessment with the child, it is the responsibility of the parents to ensure that the child is mentally and physically fit to attend school regularly. If, after admission, the child is found to be unfit to attend school due to mental or physical reasons, the management reserves the right to cancel the admission. In such cases, no refund will be issued for any payments made. .
