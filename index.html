Welcome to Delhi Public School Agra, a premier institution committed to nurturing confident, compassionate, and future-ready learners. Established in 2002, DPS Agra proudly celebrates 25 Years of Excellence (2002–2026). Functioning under the aegis of The Delhi Public School Society, New Delhi, the school promotes academic excellence, innovation, leadership, and strong values. As one of the leading CBSE affiliated schools, Delhi Public School in Agra empowers students through a learner-centric approach, experienced educators, modern infrastructure, and diverse co-curricular opportunities. As a trusted DPS in Agra, DPS Agra inspires lifelong learning and prepares responsible global citizens.
