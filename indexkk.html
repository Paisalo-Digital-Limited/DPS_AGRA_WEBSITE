About the Artist

Ilakshi is a contemporary artist specializing in portraiture, mandala designs and mixed-media canvas works. Her work blends subtle color palettes with detailed brushwork — inspired by nature and traditional motifs. She has exhibited in local galleries, taken part in art fairs and completed numerous commissioned pieces for collectors and interior designers.
