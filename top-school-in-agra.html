Why DPS Agra Is a Top Choice for Parents

DPS Agra has established itself as a respected educational institution under the aegis of The Delhi Public School Society, New Delhi. Since its establishment in 2002, the school has maintained a strong reputation for academic excellence, student achievement, and holistic development.

For many families, DPS Agra is considered a top school in Agra because it combines a strong CBSE curriculum with modern learning practices, experienced educators, and diverse co-curricular opportunities.

Academic Track Record

Academic excellence remains one of the defining strengths of DPS Agra. The school follows the CBSE curriculum and focuses on conceptual understanding, critical thinking, and practical application of knowledge.

Students are encouraged to achieve their full potential through innovative teaching methods, continuous assessment, and academic guidance. This commitment to quality education has helped DPS Agra earn recognition as a top CBSE school in Agra among parents and educators alike.

Experienced Faculty and Safe Campus

A strong faculty plays a crucial role in shaping student outcomes. DPS Agra's experienced teachers serve as mentors who guide students academically and personally.

Sports, Clubs and NCC

Education at DPS Agra extends beyond textbooks. Students have access to a variety of sports, clubs, cultural activities, leadership programmes, and National Cadet Corps (NCC) opportunities.
